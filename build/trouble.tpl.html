Silvia Troubleshooting

Symptom first. Each one lists its likely causes in order and the checks that tell them apart. Check IDs like d4 are on the power-on checks; run IDs like AC5 open on the wiring reference.

Unplug before you look inside

Every check here is done with the cord out of the wall and the machine, unless it says otherwise. A red "Unplug now" flag means stop using the machine until it's fixed. Never bridge the safety thermostat or a thermal fuse to get it running.
