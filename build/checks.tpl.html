Silvia Power‑On Checks

Every check to pass before the rebuilt Silvia is plugged in for the first time. Type in what the meter shows and each check marks itself pass or fail. Run IDs like AC4 match the wiring reference.

Unplugged for every step

Pull the cord from the wall and from the machine's inlet before you start, and keep it out until every check passes. A basic multimeter catches shorts, open circuits and wrong connections, but it can't test insulation under voltage. For first power-on, use a GFCI-protected outlet and keep the covers on.

With the Shelly, its own checks are added and the ones it replaces drop out. Once it's plugged in, the inlet, the Shelly, the power switch and their wires are live even with the machine off.
