Barista School

Learn to pull shots for light, medium and dark roasts on a stock Silvia by surfing its temperature wave, steam silky milk for two, refill the boiler and get back to brew temperature afterwards, and keep the machine clean. A live temperature map shows where you are in the heating cycle and what a shot started now would get; a coach grades every shot, jug and clean-up.

Stock machine. Everything here is for the Silvia as it comes, before Gaggiuino. With Gaggiuino the board holds the brew temperature, so surfing is no longer needed. Puck prep and milk technique still apply; for recipes, backflushing, descaling and steam settings, follow the Gaggiuino user manual. Drawn as a 120 V Silvia V6 M; pick your version in Your Silvia and the machine, its lights and its timings follow.

The course

Short lessons, each on the practice bench below. Steps tick themselves off as you do them, and each lesson ends with up to three stars. Your progress saves in this browser.
