Silvia Install Runbook

The whole Gaggiuino install in eight phases, from photos of the stock machine to the first calibrated shot, written out step by step so you can follow it with only this page open. Each step says what you need, what to do in order, and how to check it worked. Tick a step when it's done and its instructions fold away; your place is saved, so you can put the tablet down and pick up where you left off. Set Your Silvia in the top bar and your screen below, and you'll only see the steps for your build. Before you open the machine, check you're ready with Test yourself.

Your screen

Not sure? The Gen 3.5 screen has black glass right to its edges; the Gen 3 screen's glass sits on an orange circuit board with a mounting hole in each corner (official photo). A kit sold as Gen 3.5 has the Gen 3.5 screen. Headless means no screen, just a small board you use over Wi-Fi. Until you pick, the screen steps show every version.
